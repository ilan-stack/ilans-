Overview

Creative direction for children's gaming

PlayBatch builds engaging mobile games for kids. As Senior Product Designer I owned the marketing creative pipeline end to end - art-directing characters and worlds, producing trailers and gameplay capture, and designing the brand and web presence across two flagship titles: the magic match-3 adventure Magic Matters, and the web3 collection KaHuNuts: Proof of Magic.

Magic Matters

A match-3 magic adventure

Players brew spells and potions across the world of Plum - unlocking secret castle rooms as they progress. I art-directed the in-game UI, store screenshots, characters and key art that carry the magic from the listing into the game.

KaHuNuts · Web3

Proof of Magic

A web3-to-mobile play: 10,000 juju kahunuts merging the mobile and NFT ecosystems. I designed the full coconut-tiki character collection, the brand identity and the marketing site - built on Elrond and Unity, shipping to Google Play and the App Store.
