Bites

Deliver big ideas in small Bites - a B2B platform for bite-sized video training and employee engagement, serving 10,000+ users across web, mobile app and mobile web.

Role Product Designer
Duration 2 years
Focus Product Design, Design System

Overview

Bite-sized learning for the enterprise

Bites turns company knowledge into short, story-like video playlists that employees actually watch. As product designer I owned the experience end to end - from the brand and design system to the native app, the web dashboard and the mobile-web flows - working alongside researchers and content strategists to ship a product used by 10,000+ people.

Brand & Identity

A bold, friendly brand system

A playful bird mark, a confident blue, and a tagline that says it all - "deliver big ideas in small Bites". Built as a complete system with construction grids, type and colour specs, and a component library.

The App

A native app built for watching

Sign-in, a personalised feed, playlists, and a video-first experience - designed so a new hire can learn something in the time it takes to ride the elevator.

Web Dashboard

Analytics & authoring on the web

Admins create playlists, track views and completion, and dig into discussion - the same design language carried from phone to desktop.

Onboarding & Mobile Web

Frictionless sign-up & reach

A no-app-needed mobile-web path and a sign-up flow that gets people watching in seconds - email, phone, or single sign-on.
