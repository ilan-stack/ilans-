Custom AI tools for creative and ad production teams

I design and build AI tools that sit inside a creative team's existing workflow, in After Effects, in the browser, on your own files, and take the slowest manual steps out of ad and asset production.

Who it's for

  • In-house creative studios and ad teams with repetitive production work: variations, resizing, animation, captions, spritesheets.
  • Playable-ad and game-marketing teams that need assets faster than people can draw them.
  • Agencies that want AI inside the tools designers already use, not another tab.

What you get

  • Tools inside the tools you already use. After Effects Hub puts ten AI modules (image expansion, upscaling, background removal, video generation, audio, copywriting) inside After Effects, so nobody leaves the timeline.
  • Pipelines, not just prompts. Sprite Studio turns a video into a spritesheet with AI grid detection and chroma key: hours of manual work down to minutes.
  • Design and engineering from one person. I design the interface in Figma and ship it in code, so what the team tested is what they get.
  • Honest outputs. I test tools on inputs where the right answer is nothing before trusting them on real work, and make them say when they are guessing.

How it works

  1. Look at the work. Which step in your production takes the most hours, and what files go in and out of it.
  2. Prototype that one step on your real files, inside the tool your team already uses.
  3. Test it on inputs where the answer is known, including ones that should return nothing.
  4. Ship it to the team and measure the time it saves.

Questions

Can the tools run inside After Effects or Photoshop?

Yes. After Effects Hub runs ten AI modules inside After Effects and was used daily by a 100+ person creative team. I have designed five After Effects extensions.

Can it work without sending our assets to a cloud service?

Yes. The AI Studio experiment runs 25+ open models (image, video, editing, relighting, 3D, music, speech) on one $500 secondhand PC, with zero cloud and zero subscriptions.

Do you also design the interface?

Yes. I design in Figma and ship in code. Before AI tools I spent years as a creative director and product designer, so the tool is built for the people who will use it all day.
