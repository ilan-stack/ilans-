Custom AI tools for creative and ad production teams

I design and build AI tools that sit inside a creative team's existing workflow, in After Effects, in the browser, on your own files, and take the slowest manual steps out of ad and asset production.

AI agents and workflow automation with a human in control

I build AI agents that do real work, read the calendar, act on a request, run a long task, and the controls that keep a person in charge of them: approve, pause, stop, and see what is running.

I set up open AI models on hardware you own, so images, video, speech and text are generated without your data leaving the building and without a monthly subscription per seat.
